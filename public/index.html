The report

Six questions, answered on one screen.

Every report is built from live storefront signals, so it reflects the store as it stands today — not a stale database.

◎

What they actually sell

Product count, category mix with share of catalog, price band and median, variants, tags, vendors and the newest products with images.

⚡

Whether the store is alive

A 0–100 activity score built from product freshness, launch velocity, content cadence, social footprint and storefront health.

✳

Every social profile

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Threads, Discord, Telegram and WhatsApp — with live links.

A twelve-month view of how many products went live each month. The fastest read on whether a brand is accelerating or fading.

How it works

No installs. No API keys. No data vendor.

  1. 01

    Drop the URL

    Any domain works — with or without https://. We normalize it and confirm the store really runs on Shopify.

  2. 02

    We read the storefront

    Public Shopify endpoints plus page source give us the catalog, theme, apps, market settings and every linked social profile.

  3. 03

    You get the verdict

    A scored dashboard you can share as a link or a PDF: what they sell, who they are on social, and whether they are still shipping.
